The Weekly Game

Each week we turn the stories our sources collected into two games: Case Files, a set of solo puzzles with predictions graded the next week, and Town Hall, a role-play where the week's real problems hit the organization you run. Your rank, streak and progress stay in your own browser. There are no accounts and nothing is tracked.
