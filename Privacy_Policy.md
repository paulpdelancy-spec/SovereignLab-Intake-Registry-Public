## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Deliver the reports and service tier you've subscribed to
- Process and manage your subscription and billing (via Stripe)
- Send you SMS alerts, but only if you have explicitly opted in and confirmed eligibility
- Respond to your support or content-licensing inquiries
- Maintain records required for legal compliance (e.g., CAN-SPAM, TCPA where applicable)

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not share it with third parties for their own independent marketing purposes.

We retain your subscription and contact information for as long as your subscription is active, and for a reasonable period afterward as needed for legal, tax, and business record-keeping purposes, or until you request deletion under Section 7.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from, individuals under 18 years of age. If we become aware that we have collected information from someone under 18, we will delete it.

## 7. Your Rights

You may:
- **Unsubscribe** from email delivery at any time via the unsubscribe link in every email, or by contacting us directly.
- **Opt out of SMS** at any time by replying STOP, or by contacting us directly.
- **Request access to or deletion of** the personal information we hold about you by emailing us (see Section 10).
- **Update or correct** your contact information by contacting us directly.

We will respond to verified requests within a reasonable time, consistent with applicable law.
